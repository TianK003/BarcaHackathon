## P40 — The pressed side: opponents' approach as pairwise columns of the shape model (shape features version 5)

**Belief.**
- Inside a gap, the decoder places a change of team where the shape model's log-odds cross. The model reads each team
  alone: its depth, width, compactness, and the one-sided changes of its advance. A team's shape reorganises seconds
  after a turnover, so the crossing comes late (A26: +6 / +11 frames median, upper quartiles +27 / +31.5).
- One thing is visible from the players at once, and no column of v2 reads it: **who is being run at.**
  - Defenders close down the ball carrier and the players near him. This is the definition of pressure in tracking
    analytics: the spatial and velocity pressure of Bekkers' pressing intensity, and the ~5-yard radius of the pressure
    event.
  - After a turnover, the team that lost the ball counter-presses within a couple of seconds, while the team that won
    it spreads.
- So the side whose players are being approached by opponents is the side with the ball. A switch of that side marks
  the hand-over close to when it happens, in the gaps, where nothing else says so.
- It is pairwise (a player of one team against the players of the other), so it is information that P33's aggregates
  could not reach.

**Falsified if** the gap log-loss does not fall by the advisor's margin, overall and on the hand-over gaps (the gate),
or the benchmark does not move (the bar).

**Mechanism.** One sonnet task, on P30's Task 4 pattern.

*`pipeline/evidence/shape_features.py`:*
- `VERSIONS = (1, 2, 5)`. Versions 3 and 4 stay retired.
- Version 5 is version 2's 42 columns, copied and not recomputed (versions 1 and 2 stay bit for bit), plus three
  columns per team after its twenty, and three cross columns after `adv_gap` and `n_diff`.
- The per-slot quantity. For each present slot i of team T on frame t:
  - j is i's nearest present slot of the other team;
  - `approach_i` = j's velocity component towards i, `v_j · (p_i − p_j) / |p_i − p_j|` (m/s, from `kinematics`'
    velocities, T31), when `|p_i − p_j| ≤ PRESS_RADIUS_M`;
  - it is 0 when no opponent is that close, and NaN when j has no velocity (a one-frame run).
  - It is asymmetric on purpose. The distance's own rate is the same for both players of a pair, but who moves towards
    whom is not.
- The team columns, over T's slots with a value:
  - `press_mean`: the mean of `approach_i`;
  - `press_p90`: the 90th percentile, numpy's linear rule, by the module's `_percentiles` (with 11 slots, the second
    largest);
  - `press_share`: the share with `approach_i ≥ PRESS_SPEED_MS`.
  - All three are NaN when T has no such slot.
  - The module's own rule, "means, 10th and 90th percentiles and shares, never a minimum or a maximum" (T32), is kept.
    The single most pressed player, the carrier, is the maximum and is deliberately not a column.
- The cross columns:
  - `press_gap` = `h_press_p90 − a_press_p90`;
  - `press_gap_past_2s` and `press_gap_fut_2s`, its one-sided means over the two seconds before and after the frame.
    They are built with `kinematics.window_mean` as `speed_mean_past4` is, inside the block, and NaN without a finite
    value.
  - All three are antisymmetric, so `swap` negates them like the other cross columns.
- Width and blocks: team block 23, cross block 5, width 51, `_BLOCK_OF_WIDTH[51] = 23`. No clash with 26 (block 12)
  or 42 (block 20).
- The constants are declared now and are not knobs:
  - `PRESS_RADIUS_M = 5.0`, the pressure event's ~5 yards;
  - `PRESS_SPEED_MS = 2.0`, faster than a walk (`play_features.SLOW_SPEED`'s reading);
  - `PRESS_WINDOW_S = 2.0`, v2's shortest one-sided window.
- The computation is chunked (1,024 frames × 24 × 22 pairs, as `_spread_column` chunks) and memoised per Inputs like
  the other versions.

*`pipeline/benchmark/train_shape.py`:*
- `DEFAULT_MODEL_PATHS[5] = "pipeline/models/shape_possession_v5_hgb.json"`.
- The same rows, stride, mirror and P05 settings.
- `--report` is unchanged. Its `gap_classes` cells (hand-over, same team, unknown) are the gate's.

*`pipeline/evidence/shape_possession.py`:* it already computes the version its file names. Nothing changes beyond the
version check, which must accept 5.

*Not touched:* `team_features` keeps `SHAPE_VERSION = 2`. P32's model reads exactly what it was trained on.

*Config:* `pipeline/benchmark/configs/p40/p40_v5.toml` is `p32_w030.toml` with the shape model's path set to the
version 5 file (weight 0.3, scope `"all"`). A check pins it to `p32_w030` apart from that path and the description.

**Literature** (standard searches, abstracts only):

  trajectories only, as an intermediate output; PathCRF (P20) does it with a CRF.
- No paper found uses pressure as a cue for which team has an unseen ball.

## P41 — The dead-ball onset and restart in the gaps: P38's one-sided speed changes, gated on A26's gap cells

**Belief.**
- A stoppage shows in the players before anything else, by two kinds of change:
  - a collective deceleration within a second or two of the whistle;
  - an acceleration at the restart.
- The dead-ball model reads 4-second one-sided *means* of the speed and centred slow shares. A one-sided *change* (now
  minus 1 or 2 s ago; 1 or 2 s ahead minus now) marks the moment itself, as P30's one-sided changes marked the
  hand-over.
- Where the ball is seen, the ball already marks it: into `D` −1 [−2, 0], out of `D` 0 [−3, 0]. In the gaps it does
  not: +4 [−15, +21] and +1 [−17, +16], 18% within ±5 (A26).
- It is grain-neutral: the coarse truth has the raw truth's `D` frames, so a better `D` edge lifts both truths. That
  makes it the one kind of gain that P35's frontier cannot eat.
- It also leans the right way for transfer. On our match the out-of-play ball is mostly lost near the lines (T39), so
  the players carry more of the dead-ball evidence there than on DFL.

**Falsified if** the gap onset and restart log-loss does not fall by P38's margin (the gate), or the benchmark does not
move (the bar).

**The forking path, stated before the advisor rules.**
- P38's gate was declared on the into-`D` end error. The controller ruled it to the pooled ("all") cell, and it was
  closed there: upper quartile 0 frames.
- C1 printed the gap cells only after that declaration, and reopening P38 on them is a choice made after seeing the
  table. Three things hold it in:
  1. its own gate, declared here on log-loss and not on spellF1;
  2. the bar;
  3. the fresh-seed hold.
- The design is P38's as the advisor amended it, unchanged except for the gate's cells and a restart cell added
  beside the onset cell.
- If the advisor reads this as a retry of a closed lane, it is struck. It is ranked second for this reason.

**Mechanism** (P38's text, with the trainer's real shape: `train_dead_ball.py` has no feature versions and no
`--report` today):
- `play_features.py`: `VERSIONS = (1, 2)`. Version 2 is version 1's 30 columns copied, plus `speed_d_past1`,
  `speed_d_past2`, `speed_d_fut1` and `speed_d_fut2` (34). They are differences of `speed_mean`, NaN within k of a
  block's edge, and never cross half time. `features(inputs, version=1)` and `feature_names(version)` follow
  `shape_features`' pattern. Version 1 stays bit for bit.
- `dead_ball_model.py` computes the version its file names (`feature_version`, checked as a plain int). The committed
  file is version 1.
- `train_dead_ball.py`:
  - `--features {1,2}`, with the default path for 2 `pipeline/models/dead_ball_v2_hgb.json`;
  - `MONOTONIC` unchanged (keyed by name; the new columns are unconstrained);
  - `--report --out FILE`, which scores a committed file's folds on freshly gathered rows. It needs per-row masks
    gathered beside (X, y): the ball missing in the served inputs, and the distance to the nearest true into-`D` and
    out-of-`D` change.
  - The report gains four cells:
    - **onset**: alive frames within 2 s before a true into-`D` change, and `D` frames within 2 s after it;
    - **restart**: `D` frames within 2 s before a true out-of-`D` change, and alive frames within 2 s after;
    - each cell split by ball missing and ball seen;
    - existing lines unchanged.
- `team_features` and `shape_features` call only `play_features.own_half_is_left`, so they are unaffected.
- Config: `pipeline/benchmark/configs/p41/p41_dead_v2.toml` is `p32_w030.toml` with the version 2 dead-ball file. A
  check pins it.
- Checks (`checks/p41.py`):
  - 34 unique names, with v1's 30 equal in v2;
  - known differences on a synthetic deceleration;
  - NaN at block edges and across half time;
  - a version 1 file still read as version 1;
  - the cells on a synthetic truth with known changes, the ball missing and seen;

## Considered and not proposed

- **A gap model trained on gap rows only** (the advisor's untested idea): above, under "Answers to the brief's
  questions". Under `generic` it is the same model on 41% of the data, unless its columns read the gap, and those are
  P37's (T58) or the gap's timing (T38).
- **A turnover hazard in the gaps** (P35 extended): the advisor's 1 in 15 at 02:19 still holds.
  - Shape-only features would remove the T38 objection, but not what P35 showed: a per-frame cost lets the decoder
    switch more and walks the frontier.
  - In the gaps the hazard would also read the same players the emission reads.
- **The ball extrapolated for a second into each gap edge, for P32's model.** It is the only way to put the ball's own
  information into a gap, but `generic`'s log-uniform gaps leave little for it:
  - frames within 1 s of a gap's edge are 13% of the gap frames (23% within 2 s, where a straight line has long since
    overshot the reception);
  - by P30's ratio that is about +0.002 to +0.003;
  - it would also need a third team-features version and a retrained P32.
- **Interpolation across short gaps:** on our match gaps under 2 s hold 0.1% of the missing frames.
- **A team shift by ball state** (refine, from the pipeline's own lag in gaps). A26's gap medians (+6, +11) show a
  bias, but:
  - a constant that centres a distribution 40 frames wide moves the mean absolute error by about 2 frames on a third
    of the team edges, about +0.002;
  - R030 Stage E found the whole shift worth +0.00003;
  - its constant would rest on `generic`'s random gaps (T38).
- **Pressure in P32's model where the ball is seen** (team features version 3): T59. The seen edges are within a few
  frames, and P35 showed that what moves there is the grain. If P40 passes, it is a follow-up for after the deadline,
  through `team_features`' shape block.
- **The carrier's own pressure** (the maximum over a team): it breaks `shape_features`' rule against extremes (T32), so
  P40 uses the p90, the mean and the share.
- **Counts of sprinting players per team** (forward and back): v2's `vel_adv` means and one-sided advance changes
  already carry most of it, and P33 showed what further aggregates of one team give.
- **The end of pressing as a dead-ball cue** (the mean `approach` and its one-sided changes in `play_features`): it
  needs P40's helper, so it is a follow-up if both P40 and P41 pass.
- **Larger trees, more seeds, stride 1** (P23 G): the settings are P05's, fixed and never tuned. A capacity grid would
  be a fork, and P33 suggests the limit is the information, not the capacity.
- **P36, the decoder re-chosen:** unchanged. It is a walk along the frontier that P35 just measured again.
- **A learned ball locator from the players** (Kim et al. 2023's possessor model, PathCRF, P20): the general form of
  P40. It is a sequence model, and torch is not pinned. A bigger bet for Tian if P40's cue proves real.
