# P29 to P31: a learned hold, timing-aware shape features, dead-ball-neutral team evidence

Design of 12 Nov 2026, from the review of that day. Tian asked for the three proposals to be planned and, once the
plan is approved, built unattended overnight (until 07:45 on 13 Nov), in the order P29, P30, P31: the most promising
first. Feature freeze is 13 Nov, the deadline 17 Nov.

## Why

A25 says where `main.toml`'s remaining spellF1 is: in who has the ball (a perfect team where the ball is seen would
add +0.199 raw / +0.215 coarse, in the gaps +0.180 / +0.104; a perfect dead ball only +0.028 / +0.039). The decoder's
constants are spent (R030: leave-one-match-out −0.0048 and −0.0009, six seeds cannot separate the options). The review
found three places where the evidence itself is weaker than it could be:

1. **The hold never expires.** Between two control episodes `controlled_touch` votes 0.60 for the last controlling
   team on every frame with the ball visible, for as long as it takes: through ball gaps and through stoppages. After a
   stoppage the other team restarts in 57 to 90% of the cases per DFL match (A12), so the vote is then worse than
   silence.
2. **Edges are late where only the shape model speaks.** Clean pairs have a median edge error of 0 but an upper
   quartile of +14 to +27 frames (A25); 42% of DFL's team changes happen with the ball hidden (R031); P07's time
   features are centred windows of ±1 s and ±2 s, which blur a change across the window.
3. **Team evidence pushes against `D`.** A `home` term goes to `H` and `A` and not to `D`, so an episode adds
   +0.62 nats per frame for play over `D`, the hold +0.18 and P07 up to +0.20 (A19). Who has the ball should not say
   whether it is alive.

## The bar (P29 and P30), declared before any run

Against `p27_final` (the benchmark's copy of `main.toml`) in the same run: the mean of the two truths' pooled spellF1
at least **+0.005** higher, the sign-flip p of that mean difference **at most 0.05**, and **neither truth more than
0.005 lower**. A lane with several candidates first picks one by its own declared rule (below), then that one faces
the bar; the leave-one-match-out estimate of the pick is reported beside it and decides nothing.

What the bar asks, so that a miss is read correctly: the sign-flip test has one difference per match, so seven (seeds
and halves are summed inside a match). It has 128 sign patterns and its smallest p is 2/128 = 0.016. A p of at most
0.05 means winning all seven matches, or six with a small enough loss on the seventh (p 0.031 to 0.047); two losing
matches give at least 0.0625 and miss. The +0.005 is the pooled difference, a different statistic from the per-match
mean the p is computed on. Picking among four (P29) or three (P30) candidates and then testing the pick on the same
run is not corrected for; the leave-one-match-out estimate is there to show how much of the pick's gain is the
picking.

P31 has a **guard** only: neither truth more than 0.005 lower. It was proposed as a matter of principle and is
expected to be neutral on the benchmark; whether to adopt a neutral change is Tian's call.

One retry per lane at most, and only if the advisor agent finds a specific change that the first run's evidence
points to. What the retry is cannot be declared now: it is the advisor's call, outside these mechanical rules, written
into the run's ledger before its run, logged as a retry and held to the same bar. Otherwise the lane is dropped: its
commits are reverted, the last commit that held the code is named in `docs/results.md`, and the result stays logged.

## P29: a learned hold

**Belief.** How likely the last controller still has the ball falls with the time since their last controlled touch,
and falls further once the ball has been out of sight. A vote that follows that curve is stronger where the constant
is too timid (a pass in flight) and silent or reversed where it is wrong (long after, behind a gap or a stoppage).
**Falsified if** the agreement with the truth is flat across time, or if the benchmark does not move.

**Step 0, the gate (measurement only, before any mechanism is built).** On `generic`, on the hold frames of
`main.toml`'s touch rule (ball visible, outside every kept episode, after the block's first one) where DFL's truth is
alive: the share of frames where the truth's team is the last controller's, by time since the last kept frame in ten
bins (edges 0, 0.4, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 20, 40 s, the last open) and by whether the ball was hidden at any frame in
between. **The gate passes if** the 20 cells' agreements differ from the pooled agreement by at least **0.05** on
average, each cell weighted by its frames (the frame-weighted mean absolute deviation). That asks whether a curve
would say something different from a constant on the frames where the hold actually speaks; one small extreme cell
cannot carry it. The no-gap cells' agreement by time is printed beside it, as the direct look at "flat across time".
If the gate fails, P29 is dropped there.

**Mechanism.** The 20 cells' agreements, smoothed as (agree + 1) / (frames + 2), are written to
`pipeline/models/hold_curve.json`: one curve per held-out match (from the other six) and one from all seven, the way
the tree models keep `folds` and `full`. `controlled_touch` takes `hold_curve` (the file) and `hold_scale`: on a hold
frame the vote for the last controller is `0.5 + hold_scale * (p - 0.5)`, clipped to `confidence`, where `p` is the
cell's value. Below 0.5 it is a vote for the other team. The scale exists because the decoder counts correlated frames
as independent, as the constant 0.60 already allowed for.

**Candidates and pick.** `hold_scale` 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, everything else `p27_final`'s (the shift included). The
pick is the highest mean of the two truths; among those within 0.005 of it, the smallest scale.

**Not tonight.** Trees on P24's features (ball direction, the next episode's team and distance in time). They are
the follow-up if the curve clears the bar.

## P30: timing-aware shape features

**Belief.** A team that has just won the ball starts to advance and to spread, and the team that lost it to drop and
to narrow. A centred window sees the average of before and after; one-sided windows see the change itself, so the
model can place a hand-over inside a ball gap earlier and more exactly. **Falsified if** the held-out log-loss in the
gaps does not fall, or if the benchmark does not move.

**Mechanism.** A second feature set beside the first (version 2, 42 columns): the 26 of today plus, per team, `length`
(`adv_p90 − adv_p10`), `spread` (mean distance of the team's present slots to their centroid, metres),
`adv_past_2s/5s/10s` (`adv_mean` now minus k seconds ago) and `adv_fut_2s/5s/10s` (`adv_mean` k seconds ahead minus
now); NaN within k of the block's edge, never across half time. The model file names its feature version, and
`shape_possession` computes the set its file was trained on, so today's file keeps giving today's numbers. The trainer
gets `--features 2` and writes `pipeline/models/shape_possession_v2_hgb.json`: same rows, stride, mirror augmentation
and tree settings as version 1. Training takes about five minutes; this approval covers it.

**The gate.** The pooled held-out log-loss on alive frames with the ball missing (`train_shape.py --report`, 0.3560
for version 1) is **at most 0.3510**. If not, the lane stops there (retry or drop).

**Candidates and pick.** The version 2 model at weight 0.3, 0.2 and 0.45, everything else `p27_final`'s. The pick is
the highest mean; among those within 0.005 of it, the weight nearest today's 0.3. For a pick that clears the bar,
`diagnose.py` reports the clean pairs' edge errors beside `p27_final`'s (a report).

## P31: dead-ball-neutral team evidence

**Belief.** Whether the ball is alive should be decided by the evidence about that (the dead-ball model), not by how
confident the team evidence happens to be. **Mechanism.** A decoder flag `team_neutral_dead` (default false): each
`home` term also gives `D` the larger of the two team terms, `weight * max(log 2p, log 2(1 − p))`, so team evidence
ranks `H` against `A` and leaves the better of them level with `D`. It is refused together with `restart_source`.
**One candidate**, `p27_final` with the flag on; the guard above. The decoder's other constants were chosen with the
push in place and are not re-chosen tonight; that is a known limit of this test, stated in its result.

## After the three lanes

A lane is **kept** if it cleared its bar (P29, P30). If both are kept, one combined config (the two picks together) is
run and reported against the better single pick; it is the candidate only if its mean is higher and neither truth is
more than 0.005 lower, otherwise the better single pick is. If P31 held its guard and there is a candidate, the
candidate with the flag on is run once more and reported with the guard against the candidate; P31 never enters the
candidate by itself.

For the candidate an adoption package is prepared for Tian and nothing is adopted: a config under
`pipeline/configs/candidates/`, its CSV in `evaluation/results/` (gitignored), passed through the official validator,
with its sha256. No score and no statistic of it on our match is computed tonight; the public window's check belongs to
the adoption, with Tian. The candidate keeps `p27_final`'s shift (`shift_edges`), the one it cleared the bar with; the
shift re-derived for it by R030's rule (`p27_shift.py`, on DFL) is reported beside it and not applied.
