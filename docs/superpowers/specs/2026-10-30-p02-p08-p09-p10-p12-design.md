# P02, P08, P09, P10, P12: design (2026-10-30, Tian)

Five proposals from `docs/proposals.md`, built in parallel, each on its own branch. This document says what each one
is for, what gets built, how it is tested and what decides whether it reaches `main.toml`. It holds aggregates only, like
every file under `docs/`.

## 1. Intent

Tian asked for P02, P12, P08, P09 and P10 to be planned and implemented, with parallel execution, testing and regular
commits. They are the remaining "cheap" proposals: each uses an asset or a lever the pipeline does not use yet.

| part | proposal | what it adds | how it is judged |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | P02 | more label-free measurements around each change of the truth, on the public window and on DFL | analysis only (A15): nothing is adopted |
| B | P12 | a `free_x` refiner: `X` inside possessions where the decoder is unsure | a bar declared on the benchmark (R016) |
| C | P08 | a `smart_tagging` evidence source: team votes and dead-ball votes from Smart Tagging | a label-free bar on BAR-ATH (R017) |
| D | P10 | a `whistle` evidence source: the referee's whistle from the tactical video's audio | a label-free bar on BAR-ATH (R018) |
| E | P09 | the camera's aim as features of P07's shape model, with a simulated camera on DFL | a bar declared on the benchmark (R019) |

**Why each could help.**
- P12: the scorer counts an `X` on a graded frame as a miss for recall and as nothing for precision. So an `X` where
  our label is probably wrong removes a false positive at the cost of a true positive we probably did not have.
  spellF1 forward-fills `X` with our last label, so an `X` that never touches the start of a run leaves spellF1
  exactly unchanged. This can only move macroF1, the tie-break.
- P08: 638 automatic tags name a team. They are evidence that also covers the 41% of graded frames without a ball.
- P10: fouls and offsides stop play without the ball leaving the pitch. P05 sees them only when the players slow down,
  about a second later. A whistle marks the moment.
- P09: the camera pans to follow play on every frame, including the ball gaps (corr 0.84 with the ball along the
  pitch, at 35 frames' lag; A03). Where the ball is, relative to each team, should sharpen P07's shape model in the gaps.
- P02: the remaining unknown is our truth's timing convention. More label-free descriptors around each change help a
  person, or a later proposal, understand it. Nothing is set from them.

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

**Goals.**
- Each part built, with synthetic checks, on its own branch, and committed after every tested step.
- Each scored part judged by a bar written down before its first run, as in R014.
- A part that passes reaches `main.toml`, and the merged repository regenerates `main.csv` byte for byte.

**Non-goals.**
- No P02 viewer or clips for a person (Tian chose measurements only).
- No camera zoom in P09 (Tian: leave it out).
- No PANNs or other neural audio model in P10 (Tian chose a hand-built band-pass).
- No standalone short-blip refiner in P12: the decoder's `min_possession` 35 and `min_dead` 25 already forbid blips.
- No restart-team use of the tags in P08: P17 was dropped (R014, R014b).
- No uploads. Nothing goes to the Google Form.

**Anti-goals** (each would make the work a failure even if it ran):
- Any constant, choice, threshold or adoption decided from the public window's scores, or from what a person saw in
  the video (RULES.md, `f8f2629`, T01, T27).
- Any row of match data in a log, a commit, a doc, or text an LLM reads. This covers tracking, tags, audio, homography,
  and the per-frame outputs of our sources on BAR-ATH.
- A `main.toml` that a teammate cannot regenerate byte for byte from `data/` with one command.
- Two heavy processes at once on Tian's machine (memory reaper; 0x133 crashes).
- An idea kept alive after its retry failed. One knowledge-informed retry is allowed; if it fails too, the code is
  removed and the results stay logged.

## 3. Constraints

- **Hard rules (CLAUDE.md).** No match data to an LLM, including the agents that build this. Print aggregates only.
  Never commit match data, or derived per-frame data that could reconstruct it; caches go under `data/cache/`
  (gitignored). Labels come from an algorithm. No hand labels on this match. The public window is a correctness check
  only. `evaluation/` is not modified.
- **Pseudo-labels are allowed.** The rules allow testing a source against what our own pipeline says on BAR-ATH, as long
  as an algorithm made every pseudo-label and no person decided any of them. P08's and P10's bars are built on this.
- **Reproducibility.** Every new import in `pipeline/` is pinned in `requirements-pipeline.txt` in the same commit.
  Randomness comes from the seed (`np.random.default_rng([run.seed, <own constant>])`). Two runs of the final config
  must give the same sha256.
- **The machine.**
  - One heavy process at a time: `run.py`, the trainers, `conventions.py dfl`, `selftest.py --data`, `fidelity.py`, and
    the audio decode.
  - Run them throttled: `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1` (and the OMP and MKL equivalents), below-normal priority for the
    launcher and its interpreter child, at least 3 GB free before each process, about 90 s of cool-down between
    processes, and grids in chunks of about 24 configs.
  - Synthetic selftests are light and can run anywhere.
- **Research bar** (Tian's standing rule):
  - An explainable belief, and what would falsify it.
  - A bar declared before any run.
  - A pause before any retraining.
  - A throwaway label-free feasibility sweep before building a mechanism, where one is possible.
  - One retry, then drop and remove.

## 4. How the work runs

### Branches, worktrees and data

- One branch per part, each in its own git worktree beside the main checkout: `p02-measurements`, `p12-free-x`,
  `p08-smart-tagging`, `p10-whistle`, `p09-camera-aim`.
- `data/` is gitignored, so a worktree has none. Every command in a worktree sets `MTAH_DATA_DIR` to the main checkout's
  `data/`. All parts share one data directory and one cache, which is safe because heavy runs never overlap.
- Every command uses the main checkout's interpreter, `C:\Users\Tian\Desktop\BarcaHackathon\.venv\Scripts\python`.
  P10's new pin is installed into that venv once.
- Commits follow every tested step: code plus its checks green, or a declaration, or a run's results. No AI attribution
  anywhere in git metadata (managed policy). No `Claude-Session` trailer.
- Each branch is pushed to `origin` (the private `TianK003/BarcaHackathon`, checked with `git remote -v` first) after
  its commits. Nothing goes to a public remote.

### Who does what

- Building runs in parallel: one implementer agent per part, in that part's worktree. Each writes code test-first and
  runs only light checks: the synthetic `selftest.py`, and the benchmark selftest without `--dfl` or `--data`.
- Heavy runs go through one queue that the controller runs sequentially with the throttled pattern. An implementer that
  needs a heavy run hands it to the queue and waits.

### Numbers reserved up front, so parallel branches do not collide

| part | results entry | configs |
|---|---|---|
| A, P02 | A15 | none |
| B, P12 | R016 | `pipeline/benchmark/configs/r016/` |
| C, P08 | R017 | `pipeline/configs/r017_*.toml` |
| D, P10 | R018 | `pipeline/configs/r018_*.toml` |
| E, P09 | R019 | `pipeline/benchmark/configs/r019/` |

New traps take the next free `T` number at merge time; they are renumbered if two branches added one.

### Declaring, running, adopting

1. **Declaration.** Each scored part commits its results entry's part 1 (belief, candidates, bar, veto, label-free
   checks) before any run of it. Nothing in part 1 is changed after a run.
2. **Runs.** Results go in part 2, with the commit, the command and aggregates only.
3. **Adoption** (Tian: merge if the bar passes).
   - If the bar is met, no veto fires and the label-free checks pass, the part's config becomes `main.toml`.
   - The old file is kept as a named config.
   - `selftest.py` and `selftest.py --data` are green, `validate_submission.py` passes on the new `main.csv`, and two
     runs give the same sha256.
   - Then the branch is merged into `main` and `main` is pushed.
   - **Two stops for Tian.**
     - **P09:** before retraining the shape model, the fidelity gates' results go to Tian, and retraining waits for a
       go.
     - **P08 and P10:** before `main.toml` changes. Their strength in the decoder cannot be tested anywhere (Tian's
       answer, 2026-10-30). The rest of the process applies as above.
4. **Failure.** The entry logs the verdict. One knowledge-informed retry is declared in the same way. If the retry fails
   too, the part's code is removed in a commit that says where it can be recovered, and the proposal is marked dropped.
5. **Merge order.** First come, first merged, one at a time. A branch that changes `main.toml` is rebased on the latest
   `main` before merging. If `main.toml` changed under it in a way its bar depends on, its runs are repeated against the
   new `main.toml` before it merges. This affects only E, whose reference is `main.toml`; C's and D's bars are about
   their own source.

### Task 0, on `main` before the branches: benchmarking a config that has match-only sources

P08's and P10's sources read assets a DFL match does not have, so they raise `AssetUnavailable` there, and `run.py`
stops with a `BenchmarkError`. Once either source is in `main.toml`, the benchmark could not run `main.toml` at all,
and B's and E's reference would break.

- A source module that needs a BAR-ATH-only asset declares `MATCH_ONLY = True`.
- `pipeline/benchmark/run.py`, `choose.py`'s report reading, `conventions.py latency` and `headroom.py` take
  `--drop-match-only`. The flag removes those `[[evidence]]` entries before the run. It prints which ones were dropped,
  and records them in the report, whose identity check then knows the reference ran without them.
- Without the flag, behaviour is unchanged: an error that now names the flag.
- Checks (synthetic): the flag drops exactly the declared sources and no other; the report records them; without the
  flag the error is unchanged apart from its hint; `main.toml` today is unaffected.
- This lands on `main` first, and every branch starts from it.
6. **Docs on finishing.** Each part updates, on its own branch:
   - `docs/proposals.md`: status, overview row and "suggested plan";
   - `docs/results.md`: the entry, and a scoreboard row if scored;
   - `docs/traps.md`: if something cost real time;
   - `docs/technical_document.md`: if a pipeline decision lands;
   - the stage READMEs: the component tables;
   - `site/index.html`'s Models tab: if a model or source it lists changes.

   Merge conflicts in these files are resolved by keeping both sides' entries.

## 5. The parts

### A. P02: more label-free measurements around each change of the truth (A15)

**Belief.** Our truth's timing convention is unknown (A11 reads it as a lead, not a measurement). What the tracking
shows at each change describes the convention in a way a person, and a later proposal (P16), can compare with DFL:
- whether the ball is seen;
- how fast it moves;
- where it is relative to the lines;
- how close each team's nearest player is.

**Built.** An extension of `pipeline/benchmark/conventions.py`, used the same way on `public` and on `dfl`. At each
change of the truth (types `team`, `into_dead`, `out_of_dead`), descriptors computed from `Inputs` alone:
- ball visible at the change, and within ±1 s of it;
- ball speed at the change, from positions (T31);
- the ball's signed distance to the nearest line (`kinematics.outside_m`);
- the distance from the ball to the nearest slot of the old team and of the new team (for `team` changes);
- whether the new team's nearest slot is nearer than the old team's.

Output:
- **Aggregates** per type and source go into the existing JSON: shares, medians and quartiles, and n. `compare` reports
  public-minus-DFL gaps with 90% bootstrap intervals, as it already does for lags.
- **The person's CSV** (`public_transitions.csv`, gitignored, read by no LLM) gets the new columns.
- **Printing.** The code prints the summary and no row.

**Tests.** Synthetic checks in `pipeline/benchmark/checks/conventions.py`:
- each descriptor on a hand-built scene;
- the CSV writer;
- that nothing per frame is printed.

**Judged.** Not scored. A15 records the aggregates and says what they suggest. A15 states that no constant is set from
it, and that any timing constant comes from other games (P16). P02's status becomes "done, inspection only".

### B. P12: the `free_x` refiner (R016)

**Belief.** Where the HMM's posterior for the decoded label is low, the label is often wrong. Writing `X` there turns a
false positive into nothing, at the cost of a true positive on the frames where the label was right.

To first order, abstaining on a frame predicted as class k changes F1_k by (F1_k − 2p) / (2TP + FP + FN), where p is
the chance the label is right. So it gains only where p < F1_k / 2: about 0.3 to 0.45 for our classes. The grid sits
there, provided the posterior is roughly calibrated. If the posterior is overconfident, a higher threshold may be
needed, so the grid reaches 0.55.

spellF1 cannot change if `X` never sits within a margin of a run's edges, because spellF1 forward-fills `X` with the
label before it, which is the run's own label.

**Expectation, stated now.** The decoded label's posterior is rarely below 0.45, so the gain is likely small. macroF1
only breaks exact spellF1 ties, so even a pass is worth little to the ranking. Tian chose to build it anyway, and the
bar decides.

**Falsified if** no threshold gives a pooled macroF1 gain on the benchmark above the bar.

**Built.**
- `pipeline/refine/free_x.py`, registered in `refine/REGISTRY`. Parameters:
  - `threshold`: `X` where the posterior of the decoded label is below it;
  - `margin`: frames; never `X` within `margin` of a run's first or last frame, nor at a block's edges.
- It acts on `H` and `A` runs only (P12's "inside possessions").
- It needs the decoder's posterior. Configs set `hmm`'s `posterior = true`, which leaves the Viterbi labels unchanged,
  and put `free_x` before `shift_edges`, which drops the posterior.
- `margin` is 3 frames, a fixed rule: more than `shift_edges`' largest move (2), so a shifted edge never reaches an `X`.
- With no posterior the refiner refuses to run (a clear error); it never guesses.

**Tests** in `pipeline/selftest.py` (synthetic):
- `X` lands only inside `H` and `A` runs, beyond the margin;
- spellF1 of the refined labels equals spellF1 of the input, exactly, on random label sequences and posteriors, via
  `possession_metrics`;
- block edges and kick-offs are untouched;
- `threshold` 0 returns the input exactly;
- a missing posterior raises;
- `smooth50_repro.toml` and `main.toml` are unchanged byte for byte when the refiner is absent.

**Declared (R016, part 1).**
- **Candidates.** `main.toml` with `posterior = true` and `free_x`, `margin` 3, `threshold` ∈ {0.25, 0.35, 0.45, 0.55}:
  four configs in `pipeline/benchmark/configs/r016/`.
- **Runs.** The reference is `main.toml` as it is (with `--drop-match-only` if C or D has merged by then). Profile
  `tracking` v3, 3 seeds, all 7 matches, `--truth both`; `main` must reproduce R013's figures.
- **Selection.**
  - The objective is pooled raw macroF1 on `tracking`, with a tie band of 0.002.
  - Among tied configs, the best pooled coarse macroF1 wins.
  - Any tie left over goes to the lowest threshold, which writes the fewest `X`.
  - `choose.py` gains a `--metric macro_f1` option for this; spellF1 stays its default.
- **Bar.** Pooled raw macroF1 Δ ≥ +0.002 over `main.toml`, with sign-flip p ≤ 0.05.
- **Veto.** Not adopted if:
  - any pooled spellF1 Δ is non-zero (raw or coarse; zero by construction, so non-zero means a bug);
  - the pooled coarse macroF1 Δ is below −0.002.

  Alive/dead F1 is not a veto here: it counts an `X` on an alive frame as a miss by definition, and it does not rank.
  Its Δ is reported.
- **Label-free checks on BAR-ATH.**
  - Two runs give the same sha256.
  - The share of graded frames written `X` is reported.
  - The `D` share is unchanged.
  - R014's standing check "no `X` in a graded block" is replaced, for this config only, by "`X` only inside `H` and `A`
    runs, beyond the margin".

### C. P08: the `smart_tagging` evidence source (R017)

**Belief.** Smart Tagging's phase codes name the team in possession during attacking phases, and the other team during
defensive phases. Its set-piece and goal codes mark stoppages. Where the ball is seen and a controlled touch names a
team, the tag's team vote should agree with it far more often than chance. Where SET PIECES or GOALS are tagged, P05
should mostly see a dead ball.

**Falsified if** either agreement is near the base rate.

**Built.** `pipeline/evidence/smart_tagging.py`, reading `inputs.smart_tagging` (already in frames, half-open).

The votes are fixed beforehand from what the codes mean (P08's table), never from their agreement with any truth:

| codes (upper-cased) | vote |
|---|---|
| `BUILD UP`, `PROGRESSION`, `CREATING CHANCES`, `BALL IN FINAL THIRD`, `BALL IN THE BOX`, `PLAYERS IN THE BOX`, `ATTACKING TRANSITION`, `LONG BALL` | `home`: the tagged team has it |
| `DEFENDING IN … THIRD`, `DEFENSIVE TRANSITION` | `home`: the other team has it |
| `SET PIECES`, `GOALS` | `alive`: dead |
| any other code | no vote |

- **Team names.** `Barcelona` → home, `Atletic Club` → away, `N/A` → no team vote (a set-piece tag with `N/A` still
  gives its dead vote). Codes and tag values are matched case-insensitively (T17).
- **Overlaps.** Where tags disagree on a frame's team, that frame gets no team vote. Agreeing tags give one vote, not
  two.
- **Graded frames only.** Outside the graded blocks the source says NaN.
- **Values.** c_team and c_dead are the agreements measured in R017 part 2, rounded towards 0.5 to two decimals (the
  rule `--rules` uses for P05). They are passed as required parameters, so the code holds no number of its own.
- **Two strengths.** Both are built, and Tian picks between them after the bar (section 4).
  - `spread = "frame"`: `home` = c_team or 1 − c_team on every voted frame, and `alive` = 1 − c_dead on every
    dead-voted frame. The config's `weight` is the exponent; the candidate is 0.2, by analogy with P07, the other team
    source that spans long windows.
  - `spread = "tag"`: one observation per tag, spread over its n frames as `restart_team`'s `stoppage_end` spreads
    one. Each frame gets sigmoid(logit(c) / n), so the decoder sums them back to logit(c). The weight is 1.0.
- **Off the benchmark.** The module declares `MATCH_ONLY = True` (Task 0), and a DFL match raises `AssetUnavailable`;
  the source is never run on the benchmark.

**The measurement** (`pipeline/benchmark/tag_stats.py`, or a subcommand of an existing tool, the builder's choice). It
runs on BAR-ATH and prints aggregates only:
- **Team agreement.** Among frames inside kept `controlled_touch` episodes (`conventions.TOUCH`, as `touch_agreement`
  uses), the share where the tag's team vote names the episode's team. It comes with a 90% bootstrap interval that
  resamples whole tags, since frames are autocorrelated. Also the coverage of graded frames and of ball-missing graded
  frames.
- **Dead agreement.**
  - Among the frames of SET PIECES and GOALS tags, the share where `dead_ball_model` (main.toml's file and clip, with
    its pre-kick-off rule) gives P(alive) < 0.5, with the same tag-level bootstrap.
  - The same share on all graded frames, as the base rate, and the lift (the ratio).

**Tests** (synthetic, `pipeline/selftest.py`):
- the vote table, including the defensive codes' flip;
- case handling and `N/A`;
- overlap rules;
- NaN outside graded blocks;
- the half-open frame windows;
- the source's contract;
- the bootstrap's tag-level resampling on a hand-built case.

**Declared (R017, part 1).**
- **The bar.** Two independent parts, each adopted or not on its own:
  - **Team part.** Point agreement ≥ 0.75, the lower 90% bound ≥ 0.70, and team votes on ≥ 5% of ball-missing graded
    frames. Otherwise it adds nothing where it matters.
  - **Dead part.** Point dead share ≥ 0.60, the lower 90% bound ≥ 0.50, and a lift ≥ 1.5 over the base rate.
- **The strength in the decoder.** No benchmark can decide it, since DFL has no tags. If the bar passes, two configs are
  run on BAR-ATH: `main.toml` plus the passing parts at `spread = "frame"` (weight 0.2), and at `spread = "tag"`
  (weight 1.0).
  - Tian sees, for each, the aggregates against `main.toml`: the `D` share, the possessions per half, the share of
    graded frames whose label changed, and how many of those lie in ball gaps.
  - Tian picks one, or neither, before `main.toml` changes.
  - Nothing is chosen from a score.
- **Label-free checks on BAR-ATH** for the final config:
  - two runs give the same sha256;
  - the `D` share is within 1 point of `main.toml`'s;
  - the number of possessions per half is reported;
  - no `X` in a graded block.

  The public window is scored only as a correctness check (are the votes the right way round?), never to choose.
- **Retry.** If a part fails, one retry is allowed, informed by which codes disagree. Per-code agreement is reported as
  aggregates (code name, n frames, share), which is not match data at the row level.

### D. P10: the `whistle` evidence source (R018)

**Belief.** A referee's whistle is a sustained tone at 2 to 4 kHz, well above most crowd noise. Play is dead in the
moment after a whistle: either it has just been stopped, or it is about to be restarted by a kick that takes at least
half a second to follow the whistle. So detected whistles should sit near a stoppage far more often than random times
do.

**The pseudo-labels.** The comparison is with `main.toml`'s own decoded `D` runs on BAR-ATH, as `main.toml` stands at
R018's declaration commit (recorded by its sha256). P05 sees a foul only when the players slow down, about a second
after the whistle, so a whistle counts as "near a stoppage" when a decoded `D` run overlaps [w − 1 s, w + 2 s]. It is
never measured on the exact frames after the whistle, which would count the most useful whistles (the ones P05 is late
for) against it.

**Falsified if** the detections are near a decoded stoppage no more often than the same detections shifted in time
(e.g. crowd whistling).

**Built.**
- **Audio decode.**
  - `imageio-ffmpeg` (pinned in `requirements-pipeline.txt`) gives the bundled ffmpeg binary.
  - The tactical mp4's audio is piped as 16 kHz mono float32 in chunks, never loaded whole.
  - Audio second t is frame `round(t * 25)`, with no offset (A04).
- **Features.** Per video frame (40 ms): energy in the 2 to 4 kHz band, against the total and against neighbouring bands,
  plus spectral peakiness in the band (a tone, not broadband noise). numpy only (FFT). The features are cached under
  `data/cache/audio/`, keyed by the mp4's sha256 and the feature version.
- **Detector.**
  - A whistle is a run of frames whose tonal score exceeds a threshold for at least a minimum duration.
  - The threshold and the duration are declared constants from acoustics (a whistle blast lasts ≥ 0.2 s), set before
    the feasibility sweep.
  - The detection time w is the run's first frame.
- **Source.**
  - `pipeline/evidence/whistle.py` (`MATCH_ONLY = True`): `alive` = 1 − c_whistle on [w, w + 12 frames) for each
    detection, NaN elsewhere and outside graded blocks.
  - c_whistle is the precision measured in R018 (the share of detections near a decoded stoppage, rounded towards
    0.5).
  - The 12-frame window (0.5 s) is a declared constant (section 6).
  - **Two strengths**, as P08's, for Tian's pick: `spread = "frame"` at weight 1.0 (per frame over the 12 frames),
    and `spread = "event"` (one observation per whistle, spread over its 12 frames).
  - A DFL match has no audio, so the source is never run on the benchmark.
- **Reproducibility.**
  - The tonal score is rounded (to 0.01 dB) before it is compared with the threshold.
  - The run reports, as an aggregate, how many detections lie within 0.5 dB of the threshold: the ones a different
    ffmpeg build could flip.
  - Byte-identical regeneration is guaranteed on this machine. Before the final upload, a teammate on another OS
    should regenerate `main.csv` and compare sha256s. That check is noted in the submission log's checklist, and it
    does not block adoption.
- **Feasibility first** (throwaway, label-free, before the source is written). Run the detector over the match and
  report aggregates only:
  - detections per half;
  - the distribution of lags from each detection to the nearest edge of a decoded `D` run (into or out of `D`);
  - the share of detections near a decoded stoppage ([w − 1 s, w + 2 s] overlaps a `D` run);
  - the same share for a control: the detections circularly shifted within their half by a fixed set of offsets (5 to
    60 s).

**Tests** (synthetic):
- the feature extractor on generated audio (a 3 kHz tone in noise is detected, broadband noise is not, a tone shorter
  than the minimum is not);
- frame alignment (a tone at t = 2.0 s lands on frame 50);
- chunk boundaries give the same features as one pass;
- the cache key;
- the source's contract;
- a missing audio stream gives a clear error.

**Declared (R018, part 1, before the feasibility sweep).**
- **Feasibility gate.** Proceed to the source only if all three hold:
  1. detections per half are between 10 and 120;
  2. at least 50% of detections are near a decoded stoppage;
  3. that share is at least 2× the circular-shift control's.

  If the gate fails, one retry is allowed with a changed detector, declared before it runs. If that also fails, P10 is
  dropped and its code removed.
- **The bar** for adoption, on the built source: the share of detections near a decoded stoppage is ≥ 0.70, with the
  lower 90% bound (bootstrap over detections) ≥ 0.60, and it is at least 1.5× the circular-shift control's.
- **The strength in the decoder.** As P08's: if the bar passes, both strengths are run on BAR-ATH, the same aggregates
  go to Tian (plus the number of `D` onsets that moved and their median move), and Tian picks before `main.toml`
  changes.
- **Label-free checks on BAR-ATH.** As P08's. The audio decode is deterministic across two runs (same feature cache
  sha256).

### E. P09: the camera's aim in the shape model, with a simulated camera on DFL (R019)

**Belief.** The aim gives where the ball roughly is along the pitch, even when the tracker has lost it. Combined with
the teams' shape (who is pressing forward, which way play is moving), this should sharpen P07's guess of who has the
ball in the gaps. A simulated camera on DFL that matches our camera's label-free statistics lets this be measured with
a real F1 test.

**Falsified if** either the simulated camera fails its fidelity gates, or the retrained model does not beat P07's v1
by the bar.

**Phase 1: our camera, measured (label-free, BAR-ATH).**
- **The aim.** `pipeline/inputs/` gains `inputs.camera_aim`: per frame, the pitch point under the image centre (the
  inverse of each frame's homography at pixel 960, 540, then metres), x along the pitch. It is NaN where the
  homography is invalid (never, on the tactical camera).
- **The fit.** `pipeline/benchmark/camera.py` fits a smoothed follower, aim(t) ≈ a + b · lowpass(ball_x)(t − L), plus
  AR(1) noise. The constants are fitted on BAR-ATH frames where our ball is visible.
- **Statistics for the gates:**
  - the lag of peak correlation and the peak correlation;
  - the median and p90 along-pitch error;
  - the aim's speed while the ball is visible and in our ball gaps;
  - the aim's range.
- The result is cached like the Calibration (aggregates only, nothing printed per frame).

**Phase 2: the simulated camera on DFL, and its gates.**
- **The simulator.**
  - `camera.simulate(match, seed)` drives the fitted follower with DFL's clean ball (the real camera follows the real
    ball, even where our tracker loses it).
  - It is seeded per (match, seed) and served as `MatchInputs.camera_aim`.
  - DFL's `homography` still raises `AssetUnavailable`.
- **Gates** (`fidelity.py --profile camera` or the builder's equivalent): the simulated camera, measured on the
  `tracking` v3 copies with the same code as ours, against ours.
  - Peak-lag within ±10 frames of ours.
  - Peak correlation within ±0.05.
  - Median along-pitch error within ±3 m.
  - The ratio of the aim's median speed on frames `main.toml` decodes as `D` to its median speed on frames decoded
    `H` or `A`, within ±25% of ours. The same code runs on both sides, with pseudo-labels and no truth. This is the
    camera's behaviour by the state of play, which the simulator must reproduce.
  - These tolerances are declared here, before the first simulation.
- **WARNs, not gates.** The ratio of the aim's speed in ball gaps to its speed while the ball is visible (ours 0.58 /
  1.22, A03), on `tracking` and on `context`. `tracking` v3 places its gaps blind to the play (T38), so the simulated
  camera keeps moving through them and the ratio will sit near 1 there by construction. A large gap between the two
  profiles is the T38 warning that the benchmark may overstate P09. R019's veto already includes `context`.
- **If a gate fails.** One retry, declared before it runs. Then drop.

**Pause.** The gates' results go to Tian before any retraining (the research bar). Retraining starts only on Tian's go.

**Phase 3: shape features v2 and the retrained model.**
- `shape_features` gains a version 2 with aim columns, in each team's own frame (its attacking direction, per block,
  T16):
  - the aim's advance at t + L (we look ahead offline);
  - the aim's velocity along the attacking direction over ±1 s;
  - the aim's advance minus the team's mean advance.
- `swap()` still exchanges the two teams exactly, and version 1 stays readable, so `main.toml` is unchanged until
  adoption.
- `train_shape.py --features v2` writes `pipeline/models/shape_possession_v2_hgb.json` with P05's fixed HGB settings.
  Folds and `full` are as in v1.
- **Before the benchmark runs** (declared gates, not the bar):
  - `--report`'s pooled held-out log-loss on alive frames with the ball missing beats v1's by ≥ 0.005;
  - BAR-ATH's `touch_agreement` with `full` is not below v1's 0.8251 − 0.01 (the transfer gate, R011).

**Tests** (synthetic):
- the aim from a known homography;
- the follower's fit on generated data recovers its constants;
- the simulator's determinism per seed;
- v2 features on a hand-built scene;
- `swap()` exactness with the aim columns;
- that no truth reaches the simulator or the features (the existing `test_match_inputs_hold_no_truth` walk extended to
  `camera_aim`);
- v1 output byte-identical.

**Declared (R019, part 1, before the benchmark runs).**
- **Candidates.** `main.toml` with `shape_possession` reading the v2 model, at weights {0.2, 0.3, 0.5}: three configs.
  The reference is `main.toml` as it is.
- **Runs.** Profile `tracking` v3 plus the simulated camera, 3 seeds, 7 matches, `--truth both`.
- **Selection.** R013's rule (`choose.py`, raw spellF1, tie band 0.005, then coarse; a leftover tie goes to the weight
  closest to 0.2).
- **Bar.** Pooled raw spellF1 Δ ≥ +0.005, sign-flip p ≤ 0.05.
- **Veto.** R014's: the coarse Δ, the `context` raw and coarse Δs, or the alive/dead F1 Δ below −0.005.
- **Shift.** If adopted, R012's rule re-derives the shift (`conventions.py latency`).
- **Label-free checks on BAR-ATH.** As R014's.
