## P32 — The team model where the ball is seen, retrained on `generic` and measured on spellF1 (P19's one retry)

**Belief.** Where the ball is seen and no control episode runs (passes in flight, duels, loose balls, the long tail
after a touch), the evidence is the touch rule's constant hold 0.60 and the shape model at 0.3. A tree model that reads
the ball against both teams (who is closer, closing, who can reach it first, how long since and until each team's
control, which way the ball moves relative to each team's attack) names the team better there. R021 measured that:
better than the touch rule and its hold in 7 of 7 matches (+0.028; log-loss 0.2268 against 0.4340), concentrated on
the hold frames. P29's curve tonight was a one-covariate slice of the same signal and gained +0.0054 with both truths
up. **Falsified if** the retrained model is no better than the rule where the ball is seen (the gate), or the decoder
gains nothing from it (the bar).

**Why it reopens P19, and what it departs from.** P19 was dropped at a per-frame feasibility gate that compared one
source with the decoded output of all sources (R021: +0.0160 against +0.020); no spellF1 was ever measured, and R021
named a run without that gate as a separate, declared proposal. P24 is recorded as P19's one retry with "the same model
and gate"; P32 takes P24's slot and departs from it on purpose: the per-frame statistic is printed and gates nothing,
and the decision is the bar on spellF1, the objective itself. The knowledge-informed change is P24's (the ball's
direction against each team's attack), and `generic` forces a retrain anyway. **No retry after this one: a miss at the
gate or at the bar, near or not, drops P19 and P24 for good; the code is removed and the recoverable commit named.**

**Mechanism.**

## P33 — Shape features version 3

**Belief.** P30 showed that one-sided time is where the shape signal is. Version 2 leaves out (a) the difference of
the two teams' one-sided advance changes (one advances while the other drops), which an axis-aligned tree reaches only
through a staircase of splits on two columns; (b) one-sided changes of compactness, not only of position; (c) a 20 s
horizon for the long gaps. **Falsified if** the held-out gap log-loss does not fall by P30's own margin, or the
benchmark does not move.

**Mechanism.** `pipeline/evidence/shape_features.py`: `VERSIONS = (1, 2, 3)`. Version 3 is version 2's matrix, its
columns copied (versions 1 and 2 stay bit for bit), plus per team (after the twenty) `spread_past_5s`, `spread_fut_5s`,
`width_past_5s`, `width_fut_5s` (the same one-sided rule as `adv_past_ks` / `adv_fut_ks`), `adv_past_20s`,
`adv_fut_20s` (a team block of 26), and cross (after `adv_gap`, `n_diff`) `adv_past_2s_diff`, `adv_past_5s_diff`,
`adv_past_10s_diff`, `adv_fut_2s_diff`, `adv_fut_5s_diff`, `adv_fut_10s_diff`, each the `h_` column minus the `a_`
column (antisymmetric: `swap` negates them like the other cross columns). Width 60. NaN within k of a block's edge and
where either end is NaN, never across half time. `train_shape.py`: `DEFAULT_MODEL_PATHS[3] =
"pipeline/models/shape_possession_v3_hgb.json"` and `--features` takes its choices from `VERSIONS`. The model: the same
rows, stride, mirror and tree settings as versions 1 and 2. Configs `pipeline/benchmark/configs/p33/p33_w030.toml` and
`p33_w045.toml`: `p30_w030.toml` with the version 3 file at weight 0.3 and 0.45.

**Step 0, the gate.** `train_shape.py --report --out <the version 3 file>`: the pooled held-out log-loss on alive
frames with the ball missing **at most 0.3139** (version 2's 0.3189 − 0.005, P30's margin).

**Candidates and pick.** 0.3 and 0.45; the pick is the highest mean, and within 0.005 of it 0.3.

## P34 — The version 2 shape model trained on both truths' labels

**Belief.** The objective is the mean of the raw and the coarse truth, and the shape model is trained on the raw one
only. The coarse truth relabels each short possession between two of the other team; the teams' shape cannot see a
2-second possession, so for the model those raw labels are noise and for the coarse truth they are wrong. Training on
both labels, each weighted half where they differ, trains on the objective's own target. **Falsified if** the
benchmark does not move.

**Mechanism.** `train_shape.py --labels raw|both` (default `raw`: rows, fit and artifact bit for bit today's, so
`--verify` of the version 1 file still passes). With `both`: the coarse codes from the benchmark's own construct of the
coarse truth on the same frames (the coarse truth has the raw truth's alive frames: asserted); where the two labels
agree a row has weight 1, where they differ it appears twice, once with each label, each at weight 0.5; the mirrors
likewise; the trees get `sample_weight`. The artifact gains `"labels": "both"` only then. File
`pipeline/models/shape_possession_v2both_hgb.json` (version 2 features). `--report` adds the coarse truth's log-loss
and accuracy beside the raw ones; the existing cells are unchanged. Config `pipeline/benchmark/configs/p34/p34_both.toml`:
`p30_w030.toml` with that model path.

**Step 0, a sanity gate.** On alive frames with the ball missing, from two reports in one chain (the version 2 file and
the new one, both with the coarse columns): the mean of the raw and coarse pooled log-losses at most version 2's mean −
0.003, and the raw log-loss at most version 2's raw (0.3189) + 0.010. It is close to the model's own training
objective, so it screens a broken build, not a weak idea; the bar decides.

**Candidates.** One config, weight 0.3; no pick.
