### Task 13: P35 A1, the decoder's per-frame team cost

**Files:** Modify `pipeline/decode/hmm.py`, `pipeline/decode/README.md`, `pipeline/config.py` (the cross-check
between `hazard_source` and the `turnover_hazard` sources), `pipeline/benchmark/diagnose.py` (`posterior_parameters`
and the calibration loop only: refuse a hazard config in the posterior block, leave the hazard source out of the
calibration table), `pipeline/selftest.py` (after the `test_hmm_*` checks), `pipeline/benchmark/checks/p35.py`
(functions `test_p35_decoder_*` only).

**Interfaces:** the spec's "P35, Mechanism, A1", with amendments 4 and 11:
- `decode(ctx, ..., hazard_source=None, hazard_scale=None)`; `hazard_scale` None means 1.0 with a source; refused:
  a scale without a source, a negative or non-finite scale, a hazard with `restart_source` or with
  `team_neutral_dead`, `posterior = true` with a hazard (until `marginals` takes `team_cost`, amendment 11d).
- The named source contributes nothing to the emissions (`log_likelihoods(..., skip=name)` or equivalent); its `home`
  is z.
- `team_cost[t] = clip(switch_team − hazard_scale · logit(clip(z_t, eps, 1 − eps)), HAZARD_FLOOR, 2 · switch_team)`
  for t ≥ 1; NaN z_t gives `switch_team`; `HAZARD_FLOOR = 1.0` (a module constant).
- `viterbi(..., team_cost=None)` / `_best_path(..., team_cost=None)`: None runs today's loop untouched (the per-frame
  path chosen once, before the loop); an array: the H ↔ A predecessor of a run entered at t costs `team_cost[t]`, D
  changes cost `switch_dead` as before, the tie order unchanged.
- `pipeline/config.py`, after the components are built: an hmm decoder's `hazard_source` must name an evidence
  component of kind `"turnover_hazard"`, and every evidence component of kind `"turnover_hazard"` must be named by the
  decoder's `hazard_source` (a `ConfigError` naming both). Tested with a stand-in registry (the real source arrives with
  A2).

- [ ] Step 1: failing checks: (a) every existing hmm check unchanged; (b) `team_cost` filled with `switch_team` gives
  labels identical to None on random blocks and on exactly tied gap blocks; (c) brute force over every valid path of
  tiny blocks (n ≤ 8, `min_possession` 1 to 3, `min_dead` 1 to 2, random continuous emissions and costs) matches
  `viterbi`'s labels and score; a hazard source NaN everywhere, and `hazard_scale` 0, give labels identical to no
  hazard; a flat-evidence stretch between two teams' runs with a hazard peak at frame k puts the change on k; the
  refusals; the config cross-check both ways; 80,000 frames within 1.5 times today's decode time.
- [ ] Step 2: implement; Step 3: both self-tests pass.
- [ ] Step 4: commit `"P35: the hmm decoder's per-frame team cost from a named hazard source (hazard_source, hazard_scale), off by default"`.
- [ ] Optional second commit: `marginals(..., team_cost=None)` with its own brute force, lifting the posterior refusal.

**Controller:** merge first among the P35 pieces' decoder side (after C1); main_check, `selftest.py --data`, the
benchmark self-test.
