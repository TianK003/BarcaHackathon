## Decisions the advisor must sign off before Task 1 (D1 to D4)

The advisor's rule 4 is binding. PathCRF's code forces four completions that it does not state (research.md §3, §8).
The controller sends this section to the advisor and records the answer as "Advisor (for Tian): …" in R022 part 1.
If the advisor changes one, Task 1 declares the advisor's version, and Tasks 6 to 9 implement it.

- **D1, segmentation.** The model was trained and is run only inside in-play episodes, and its four outside nodes are
  absorbing. A window that starts in dead time can therefore stay "out of play" through the restart. Truth episodes
  would put the truth into the inputs.
  - **Proposed:** windows tile the maximal runs that `main.toml` (pinned, `--drop-match-only`) decodes as H or A
    inside each graded block, on that match and seed. That is label-free, and it is step 1's own segmentation on
    BAR-ATH.
  - A run of L frames becomes ⌈L/250⌉ windows of near-equal length (at most 250 frames, 10 s).
  - A run shorter than 16 frames gets no window: their kinematics need 16 frames for `savgol(15)`.
- **D2, complete tracks.** `inference_episode` skips any window with a NaN in an active node.
  - **Proposed:** a join's gap is filled by linear interpolation. Inside the window, a node's frames before its first
    observed frame and after its last are held at that position.
  - Their `calculate_running_features` (25 Hz Savitzky–Golay, the 12 m/s outlier repair) then runs on each window's
    node tracks.
  - x × 105 and y × 68: their standard pitch.
- **D3, per-window inference.** Node identities differ between windows, so their 50% overlap blending and the
  episode-wide Viterbi do not apply.
  - **Proposed:** each window is its own episode for `inference_episode` (`use_crf=True`, `decode="indep"`,
    `window_seconds=10`, `last_node=None`): one model pass and one CRF Viterbi per window.
  - That matches training: 10 s windows with the CRF loss computed per window.
  - The decoded 5-fps state is held forward to 25 fps by their own `ffill` ("5 fps held 5 frames").
- **D4, the team of a frame, and the frame set.**
  - **The team.** A decoded edge's team is its **sender's**: the self-loop's player, or the passer while the ball is in
    flight (DFL's flag convention). It is found by the node's name (`home_k` / `away_k`), never by block, because
    blocks are left and right.
  - **Wrong frames.** An outside sender is out of play and counts as **wrong** (rule 5). A frame with no PathCRF output
    (silent) also counts as wrong.
  - **Primary frames:** rule 5's frames (graded, truth H/A, the seed's served ball missing) **∩ `main.toml` decodes H
    or A** on that seed. Neither model is charged for `main.toml`'s dead-ball errors, which is the advisor's own rule
    for P19's comparator.
  - The literal rule-5 set, with silent frames wrong, is reported beside it.

## Task 6: the adapter, part 1: segmentation, pieces, joins, nodes (pure numpy)

**Files:** write `pathcrf_lab/adapter.py`; test in `checks_main.py`.

**Interfaces** (numpy only, no truth anywhere):
- `# Params`:
  - `MAX_WINDOW_FRAMES = 250`, `MIN_RUN_FRAMES = 16`;
  - `MAX_JOIN_FRAMES = 50` (last seen to first seen, 2 s at 25 fps), `MAX_JOIN_M = 5.0`;
  - `PER_TEAM = 11`, `PITCH = (105.0, 68.0)`.
- `alive_runs(codes, graded) -> (runs, short)`.
  - `runs` is a list of `(start, stop)`, half-open: the maximal runs of `pm.H` or `pm.A` inside each graded block.
  - A run under `MIN_RUN_FRAMES` goes to `short` (a count and its frames).
- `split_run(start, stop) -> list[(start, stop)]`: ⌈L/250⌉ windows of near-equal length, the longer ones first.
- `pieces(present, start, stop) -> list[Piece(slot, first, last)]`: the maximal present runs per slot inside the
  window. `last` is inclusive.
- `join(pieces, xy_m, slot_team) -> (chains, n_joins)`.
  - Candidates are pairs (a, b) in the same metadata team with `1 <= b.first - a.last <= MAX_JOIN_FRAMES` and
    `|xy_m[b.first, b.slot] - xy_m[a.last, a.slot]| <= MAX_JOIN_M`.
  - They are taken greedily in the order (gap, distance, a.slot, b.slot, a.last). Each end is used once and each
    start once; chains are allowed.
- `nodes(chains, xy_m, start, stop, slot_team) -> Nodes`.
  - Per team (0 home, 1 away), keep at most 11 chains. Drop those with the fewest observed frames; on a tie, drop the
    chain with the higher first slot.
  - A kept chain becomes a (T, 2) track: linear across join gaps, held at the window's edges.
  - `Nodes` has `xy` (T, 22, 2) float32: home in 0–10, away in 11–21, NaN on unused rows.
  - It has `team` (22,) int8, −1 for unused, and `names` (`home_0…`, `away_0…`, `""` for unused).
  - It has `flags`: `joins`, `over11` (some frame of the window has more than 11 present pieces of one metadata
    team), `dropped`, `padded_home`, `padded_away` and `held_frames`.
- `window_nodes(atd, start, stop) -> Nodes`. It converts `atd.players_xy` (normalised) to metres with x × 105 and
  y × 68, then applies `pieces`, `join` and `nodes`.

## Review focus

1. **The truth never reaches the windows.**
   - `adapter.py` takes the match only through `served_inputs`, and the segmentation only from `main.toml`'s decoded
     codes.
   - `runner.py` never sees a truth array.
   - `score.py` is the only reader. Check `test_adapter_reads_no_truth`, and grep the three modules.
2. **Frame alignment.**
   - A window's rows equal the match rows: `frame_id = rows` in `window_frame`, and `set_index("frame_id")` before
     `inference_episode`.
   - The 5-fps hold comes from their `down_idx` (every 5th row plus the last) and `ffill`. One frame off shifts every
     code.
   - Clean frames map by DFL's frame number N through `match.frame_number`, never by position, and the alive flags
     must agree on 100% of mapped rows.
3. **Team by name, not by block.** The model's blocks are left and right. A window where home attacks from the right
   must still give home codes for `home_*` senders, and pads take their name's team. A wrong mapping would invert half
   the windows and still look plausible.
4. **The join rule exactly as declared.** It is inclusive at 50 frames and 5.0 m, within one metadata team, greedy in
   the declared order, with each end and each start used once. Wrong-team slots stay in their metadata team, and the
   extras dropped are the fewest-frames ones.
5. **Wrong is wrong.** Out of play (2) and silent (−1) are wrong for PathCRF, and NaN home is wrong for P07. The
   verdict compares exact fractions of integer counts: +0.0399 is a no-go, and exactly +0.04 is a go. Floats would
   get the second wrong.
6. **The reference reproduces before anything is read.** 96,442 / 0.8495 on the literal set, scored as R011 scored
   it (silent left out). If not, the lane stops.
7. **Isolation and safety.**
   - Nothing in `pipeline/` or either selftest imports `pathcrf_lab`.
   - No torch at module import.
   - `weights_only=True` and one thread.
   - PathCRF's files are pinned by commit, blob SHA-1 and sha256, and none is committed.
   - `.venv-pathcrf/` is ignored, and the pipeline's requirement files are unchanged.
8. **Pins.** `prepare` refuses a `main.toml` or a shape model whose sha256 is not part 1's, because P22 or P19 may
   change `main.toml` tonight.
9. **Aggregates only.** No printed line, report or commit holds a frame-level array of DFL or anything derived from
   BAR-ATH: the Calibration's masks travel inside the degraded Atd.
