# P19: a learned team model where the ball is seen (R021)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development or superpowers:executing-plans.
> Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax. Every step marked **HEAVY** is the controller's: implementers write the code and
> the exact command, then stop at that step.

**Goal:** train boosted trees on the seven DFL matches (degraded as `tracking` v3) to say which team has the ball on
the frames where our tracking sees it. Serve the trees as a `home` source, `team_model`. Run three gates before the
model can enter `main.toml`, all declared in R021 part 1 before any governed run:
- a feasibility go/no-go against `main.toml`'s own labels;
- a two-stage grid, the bar and a leave-one-match-out (LOMO) estimate;
- a label-free transfer check on BAR-ATH.

**Architecture:** the source follows P07's pattern, and nothing changes `pipeline/contracts.py`.
- `pipeline/evidence/team_features.py`: 56 per-frame columns, numpy only.
- `pipeline/evidence/team_model.py`: the source. It walks the JSON trees with `trees.py` and symmetrises them,
  logit = (r(x) − r(swap(x))) / 2.
- `pipeline/benchmark/train_team.py`: the trainer. It reuses P05's `HGB_SETTINGS`, the export and `--verify`, and adds
  `--dry-run` and `--feasibility`.
- `pipeline/benchmark/team_grid.py`: the grid's configs, written from `main.toml`'s text, and the bar's verdict.
- `pipeline/benchmark/team_transfer.py`: the BAR-ATH agreement with Smart Tagging's votes, in aggregates.

**Tech stack:**
- Python 3.11.9 with numpy and pandas in `pipeline/`. scikit-learn appears only under `pipeline/benchmark/`, as pinned.
- No new dependency. The peak-memory helper uses stdlib `ctypes` and `resource`.
- There is no test framework: checks are plain `test_*` functions, collected by `pipeline/selftest.py` and
  `pipeline/benchmark/selftest.py`.

## Review focus

1. **Truth through the inputs.** `team_features` reads only `Inputs`. The trainer reads the label from
   `truth.truth_codes(match)` beside the features, never through them (`checks.common.leaks`).
2. **Antisymmetry.** `swap` is exact: the two team blocks trade places, the 12 shared columns stay, and the 26 shape
   columns go through `shape_features.swap`. Relabelling the slots' teams of a scene gives `swap(X)`, and the source
   gives 1 − p to 1e-12.
3. **Speeds from positions only (T31).** Garbage in `atd.ball_speed` or `atd.players_speed` changes no column.
4. **Blocks (T11).** No window, episode, gap run or closing speed reads across a graded block's edge. Rows outside the
   blocks are all-NaN.
5. **Where the source speaks.** `home` is NaN wherever the ball is missing and outside the blocks. `alive` is NaN
   everywhere.
6. **Out of fold.** A benchmark match is scored by its own fold. `--feasibility` refuses an artifact that lacks a fold
   (it would score in sample).
7. **The feasibility's frames and arithmetic.**
   - The frames are graded, truth H/A, served ball visible and `main.toml` decoded H/A.
   - The rule's silent frames count as wrong.
   - The go is the integer test 50·(p19_right − main_right) ≥ frames.
8. **Grid configs are `main.toml` verbatim** apart from the intended lines (`load_config` round-trip check). The tie
   order the stems are passed in is the declared one.
9. **Existing behaviour is unchanged:** no existing module edited, and `main.toml`'s sha256 the same before and after
   (HEAVY, at the pause).
10. **The touch rule in the features is frozen** at `main.toml`'s 1.5 m / 6 m/s / 1 frame, by `controlled_touch`'s
    own code. A check compares it with `main.toml`.

## Task 5: the feasibility test, `train_team.py --feasibility`

**Interfaces** (in `train_team.py`):
- `feasibility(artifact, config_path, profile="tracking", seeds=3, matches=None, calibration=None, data_dir=None) ->
  dict`.
  - The config is loaded, then `run.drop_match_only`.
  - Per match and seed:
    - `codes = run.predict(config, inputs)[0]`;
    - P19's `home` from `team_model.compute` on the artifact written to a temporary file (as `train_shape.report`);
    - the rule from `controlled_touch.compute(**config's controlled_touch params)`, which carries hold 0.60 and
      confidence 0.93 from the file, not retyped.
  - Frames: graded ∧ truth H/A ∧ visible ∧ codes ∈ {H, A}. The coarse cell uses `truth.coarse_codes` on its own frames.
- A cell is
  `{frames, p19: {right, accuracy, log_loss}, main: {right, accuracy}, rule: {right, accuracy, silent, log_loss},
  coarse: {frames, p19, main, rule}, classes: {episode, hold, silent, over11, not_over11: {frames, p19, main}}}`.
  - The rule's log-loss scores a silent frame at 0.5.
  - The classes come from the rule's value (confidence, hold or NaN) and from `h_over11`/`a_over11`.
- `go(cells) -> {"wins", "pooled", "go"}`: a match wins iff `50 * (p19_right - main_right) >= frames`. Go iff wins ≥ 6
  and the same holds pooled.
- A missing fold raises `BenchmarkError` (it would score in sample). The output holds no per-frame array.
- The CLI prints the table and the verdict and writes `<data>/runs/r021/feasibility.json`.
