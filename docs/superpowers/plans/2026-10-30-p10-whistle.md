**Goal:** detect the referee's whistle in the tactical video's audio with a hand-built band-pass detector, check it
label-free against our own decoded stoppages (feasibility gate, then bar), and turn it into `alive` evidence. If it
passes, Tian picks the strength before `main.toml` changes.

**Spec:** design section 5.D (pseudo-labels with the [w − 1 s, w + 2 s] allowance, the gate, the bar, rounding,
strengths).

**Branch / worktree:** `git worktree add ../BarcaHackathon-p10 -b p10-whistle main` (after Task 0).

**Data rule for this part:** the audio is match data.
- Never write, play or print samples, spectra or per-frame features.
- Print and commit only counts, shares, medians and quartiles, hashes and pass/fail.
- The feature cache lives under `data/cache/audio/`.

- Test: `pipeline/selftest.py`

**Interfaces:**
- `# Params`:
  - `SAMPLE_RATE = 16000`;
  - `HOP = 640` (one video frame at 25 fps);
  - `WINDOW = 1024` (Hann);
  - `BAND = (2000.0, 4000.0)`;
  - `NEIGHBOURS = ((1000.0, 2000.0), (4000.0, 6000.0))`;
  - `FEATURE_VERSION = 1`;
  - `FEATURE_NAMES = ("tonal_db", "band_db")`;
  - `CHUNK_S = 60`.
- `features_from_samples(samples: np.ndarray, n_frames: int) -> np.ndarray` (n_frames, 2), float64, pure numpy.
  - Frame k's window is centred at `(k + 0.5) * HOP` samples, i.e. samples
    `[k*HOP - (WINDOW - HOP)//2 , k*HOP - (WINDOW - HOP)//2 + WINDOW)`, zero-padded outside the signal.
  - `tonal_db` = 10·log10(the band's peak bin power / the band's median bin power).
  - `band_db` = 10·log10(the band's mean power / the neighbours' mean power).
  - Use a small floor (1e-12) inside the logs, so silence gives finite values.
  - Round both to 0.01 dB (design 5.D, reproducibility).
- `decode_features(mp4_path: str, n_frames: int) -> np.ndarray`.
  - Run the `imageio_ffmpeg.get_ffmpeg_exe()` binary with `-nostdin -hide_banner -loglevel error -i <mp4> -vn -ac 1
    -ar 16000 -f f32le -acodec pcm_f32le -threads 1 -`.
  - Read stdout in `CHUNK_S` chunks plus the window's overlap, so the result equals `features_from_samples` on the
    whole signal.
  - No audio stream: `ValueError` naming the file and saying it has no audio stream.
- `load_or_build(mp4_path, n_frames, cache_dir) -> np.ndarray`, read-only.
  - The cache is `<cache_dir>/audio/<key>/features.npy` plus `manifest.json`.
  - The key is the sha256 of `(mp4 sha256, FEATURE_VERSION, SAMPLE_RATE, HOP, WINDOW, BAND, NEIGHBOURS)` as canonical
    JSON. The mp4's sha256 is itself cached in the manifest by (size, mtime), so it is computed once.
  - The manifest also holds the features' sha256 (for the determinism check).
- `Inputs.audio_features`: `load_or_build(<the tactical mp4 from games.require>, self.n_frames, self.cache_dir)`.
  Find the mp4's key in `games.asset_paths`; never hard-code its name.

## Task D.2: the detector, R018 part 1, and the feasibility sweep

**Files:**
- Create: `pipeline/evidence/whistle.py` (the detector part now; `compute` in Task D.3)
- Create: `pipeline/benchmark/whistle_stats.py`
- Test: `pipeline/selftest.py` (detector) and `pipeline/benchmark/checks/whistle_stats.py`

**Interfaces:**
- `whistle.py` `# Params`: the declared detector constants, argued from acoustics in the docstring, fixed here before
  any run:
  - `TONAL_DB = 15.0` (a whistle's partial stands well above the band's median bin in crowd noise);
  - `BAND_DB = 3.0` (broadband crowd noise leaves the 2–4 kHz band about 2 dB below its neighbours; a whistle lifts
    it);
  - `MIN_FRAMES = 5` (0.2 s: a blast is longer);
  - `WINDOW_FRAMES = 12` (0.5 s of dead after a whistle).
- `whistle.detect(features: np.ndarray, graded_mask: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray`.
  - It returns the int64 first frames w of the runs of at least `MIN_FRAMES` graded frames where `tonal_db ≥ TONAL_DB`
    and `band_db ≥ BAND_DB`.
  - A run never crosses a block edge.
- `whistle_stats.near_stoppage(w, dead, blocks) -> bool array`: True where a decoded `D` frame lies in
  [w − 25, w + 50] inside w's block.
- `whistle_stats.feasibility(w, dead, blocks, *, offsets_s=(5, 10, ..., 60)) -> dict` with:
  - `per_half` counts;
  - `share_near`;
  - `control_share_near`: the mean over the offsets of the share after circularly shifting each half's detections by
    the offset within that half;
  - `ratio`;
  - `lag_to_edge` quartiles: the frames from w to the nearest edge of a decoded `D` run.
- `whistle_stats.bar(w, dead, blocks, *, draws=2000, seed=(18, 0)) -> dict`: `share_near` with a 90% bootstrap over
  detections (`low`, `high`), the control's share, and the ratio.
- CLI: `$PY pipeline/benchmark/whistle_stats.py feasibility|bar [--data-dir D]` (HEAVY).
  - It runs `pipeline/configs/main.toml` on BAR-ATH through `pipeline.run`'s functions to get the decoded `D`, and
    prints `main.toml`'s sha256 and the aggregates only.
  - It also reports how many detections have a feature within 0.5 dB of its threshold (design 5.D).
  - It writes `data/runs/whistle/<mode>.json`.

## Task D.3: the source, the bar, the strengths

  `pipeline/evidence/README.md`

**Interfaces:**
- `MATCH_ONLY = True`.
- `compute(run, *, c_whistle, spread="frame") -> Evidence`.
  - `c_whistle` is a float in (0.5, 1]. `spread` is "frame" or "event".
  - `w = detect(run.inputs.audio_features, run.inputs.graded_mask)`.
  - `alive` on [w, w + `WINDOW_FRAMES`), cut at the block's end: "frame" gives 1 − c_whistle; "event" gives
    1 − sigmoid(logit(c_whistle) / n) with n the window's frames after cutting.
  - Overlapping windows take one value (frame) or the sum of their logits (event).
  - `home` is NaN everywhere, and so is `alive` outside the windows and outside graded blocks.
