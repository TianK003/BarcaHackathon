## Task E.2: our camera's fit, and the simulator

**Files:**
- Create: `pipeline/benchmark/camera.py`
- Modify:
  - `pipeline/benchmark/run.py`: where the Calibration is loaded for a non-clean profile, also
    `camera.load_fit(data_dir)`. Each `MatchInputs` then gets `camera_aim=camera.simulated_aim(match, seed, fit)`. The
    report gains `"camera": {"sha256": <fit digest>}`.
  - `pipeline/benchmark/train_dead_ball.py`: `served_inputs` does the same, so the trainers and `conventions.py` serve
    it too.
- Test: `pipeline/benchmark/checks/camera.py` (new, collected like the others)

**Interfaces:**
- `@dataclass(frozen=True) CameraFit`: `lag` (frames), `tau` (frames), `a`, `b` (metres, slope), `phi`,
  `sigma` (metres), `lo`, `hi` (metres, the aim's 0.5th and 99.5th percentiles), `stats` (dict of aggregates for the
  gates).
- `lowpass(x: np.ndarray, tau: float) -> np.ndarray`: a first-order exponential smoother, alpha = 1 / (1 + tau). It
  holds the last value across NaN, and restarts at each block start.
- `fit(aim: np.ndarray, ball_x: np.ndarray, blocks) -> CameraFit`.
  - For each `lag` in 0..80 and `tau` in (0, 5, 10, 20, 35, 50), fit aim(t) = a + b · lowpass(ball_x)(t − lag) by
    least squares on frames where both are finite, and keep the pair with the smallest residual variance. Ties go to
    the smaller lag, then the smaller tau.
  - The residuals' lag-1 autocorrelation gives `phi`, and `sigma = sd · sqrt(1 − phi²)`.
  - Deterministic; no randomness.
- `measure(aim, ball_x, ball_seen, dead) -> dict`: the gate statistics, the same code for ours and for the
  simulation, with `dead` as a boolean array of decoded `D` frames.
  - `peak_lag` and `peak_corr`: the lag 0..80 maximising corr(aim(t + lag), ball_x(t)) on frames with the ball seen.
  - `median_err`: the median of |aim(t + peak_lag) − ball_x(t)|.
  - `speed_ratio_dead_alive`: the aim's median |Δaim| · fps on `dead` frames over the same on non-dead graded frames.
  - `speed_ratio_gap_visible` (the WARN): the same, on frames without the ball over frames with it.
- `simulated_aim(match, seed, fit) -> np.ndarray`.
  - Drive the fitted follower with the clean ball, `clean_atd(match).ball_xy[:, 0] · pitch length`: lowpass, shift by
    `lag`, `a + b·`.
  - Add AR(1) noise from `np.random.default_rng([seed, 9009, zlib.crc32(match.match_id.encode())])`, and clip to
    [`lo`, `hi`].
  - NaN outside the graded blocks.
- `load_fit(data_dir) -> CameraFit`.
  - It fits on BAR-ATH (`Inputs().camera_aim`, `kinematics.ball_track(inputs)` x, `inputs.graded`) once, and caches
    under `data/cache/benchmark/camera/<digest>/fit.json`. The digest is the sha256 of the ATD and homography sources
    plus `camera.py`'s fit-relevant constants.
  - `digest(fit) -> str` is for reports.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** (`checks/camera.py`, synthetic):
  - `test_camera_fit_recovers_known_constants`: data generated by the follower with lag 30, tau 10, a 5, b 0.6, phi
    0.95 and sigma 2 (seeded) give back lag and tau exactly, and a, b, phi and sigma within 5%.
  - `test_camera_simulation_is_deterministic_per_seed`: the same (match, seed) gives identical bytes; another seed
    differs; NaN outside the graded blocks; clipped to [lo, hi].
  - `test_camera_measure_on_a_hand_built_scene`: exact `peak_lag`, `median_err` and both ratios.
  - `test_camera_fit_prints_aggregates_only`.
  - `test_runner_serves_camera_aim_off_clean`: a synthetic run under a non-clean profile serves `camera_aim`, under
    clean does not, and the report names the fit's digest. Existing configs' scores are byte-identical.
- [ ] **Step 2:** run, expect FAIL. **Step 3:** implement. **Step 4:** run, expect PASS.

## Task E.3: the gates

**Files:** `pipeline/benchmark/camera.py` (a CLI) and `docs/results.md`.

**Interfaces:**
- CLI: `$PY pipeline/benchmark/camera.py gates [--profile tracking] [--seeds 3] [--data-dir D]` (HEAVY).
  - **Ours:** `measure` on BAR-ATH, with `dead` from `main.toml`'s decoded labels (run through `pipeline.run`'s
    functions).
  - **Simulated:** `measure` pooled over the 7 DFL matches × seeds. It uses the served (degraded) ball for `ball_seen`
    and `ball_x`, the simulated aim, and `dead` from `main.toml` decoded on each served match (`run.predict`). No
    truth is used.
  - The gates, declared in the spec:
    - |Δ`peak_lag`| ≤ 10;
    - |Δ`peak_corr`| ≤ 0.05;
    - |Δ`median_err`| ≤ 3.0 m;
    - |sim / ours − 1| ≤ 0.25 for `speed_ratio_dead_alive`.
  - The WARN is `speed_ratio_gap_visible`, on the given profile (run it on `tracking` and on `context`).
  - It writes `data/runs/camera/gates_<profile>.json`, prints PASS / FAIL per gate, and exits 1 on a failed gate.

  read at t + LOOKAHEAD.
