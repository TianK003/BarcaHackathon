### Task 1: `choose.py --objective mean` (R024's rule)

**Files:**
- Modify: `pipeline/benchmark/choose.py`
- Test: `pipeline/benchmark/checks/choose.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `choose.choose(stems, reference, profile=..., band=TIE_BAND, data_dir=None, match_ids=None, seeds=None, metric="spell", objective="raw")`; `choose.OBJECTIVES = ("raw", "mean")`; CLI `--objective raw|mean` (default `raw`). With `objective="mean"` the result also has, per row, `rows[stem]["mean"] = {"spell": .., "macro": .., "per_match": {...}, "delta", "p", "wins"}` (the means of the raw and coarse entries; `per_match` of the chosen `metric`), `versus["mean"]` with the keys `score.compare` returns (`pooled_delta`, `match_deltas`, `mean_delta`, `low`, `high`, `p_sign_flip`, `wins`, `losses`, `n`), lomo deltas under `"mean"` too, and `"objective": "mean"` at the top level. With `objective="raw"` the result is today's plus `"objective": "raw"`.

The rule with `objective="mean"`, from R024 part 1:
- objective(stem) = (raw + coarse) / 2 of the metric's pooled score;
- best = the highest objective, the first in the given order on an exact tie;
- tied = every stem with objective ≥ best − band;
- chosen = the tied stem with the highest min(raw, coarse), the first in the given order on an exact tie;
- `versus["mean"]`: `pooled_delta` = the mean of the raw and coarse `pooled_delta`s; `match_deltas[m]` = the mean of the two truths' delta on match m; `mean_delta, low, high = score.t_interval(deltas)`, `p_sign_flip = score.sign_flip_p(deltas)`, wins and losses beyond `score.FLIP_TOLERANCE`;
- lomo: per held-out match, each stem's six-match mean of its per-match objective; the "objective" rule picks the best of those (order on a tie); the "rule" picks by the band, then the six-match min(raw mean, coarse mean), then the order; held-out deltas reported for raw, coarse and mean.
- `render` prints `objective mean` in the header, a mean column and its Δ / p / wins, and "tied" lines with each stem's mean and min. `objective="raw"` prints exactly today's lines except the header's new `objective raw` field.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `checks/choose.py`, on the synthetic reports `written()` makes:
  - `test_choose_mean_objective_applies_r024s_rule`: for band 0.0 and 1.0, `best`, `tied` and `chosen` equal a plain recomputation from `score.pooled` per truth (objective = mean; maximin; order); `versus["mean"]["pooled_delta"] == (versus["raw"]["pooled_delta"] + versus["coarse"]["pooled_delta"]) / 2` within 1e-12; `versus["mean"]["match_deltas"]` is the per-match mean; `p_sign_flip == score.sign_flip_p(list(match_deltas.values()))`.
  - `test_choose_mean_lomo_matches_a_plain_loop`: both lomo rules' picks and their mean-objective deltas recomputed in a plain loop.
  - `test_choose_mean_maximin_breaks_a_tie`: a constructed `rows` (call `choose._select_mean` or the helper you write) where two stems tie on the mean within the band and the one with the larger min(raw, coarse) wins even though it is named second; and an exact tie on both goes to the first named.
  - `test_choose_raw_objective_is_unchanged`: with `objective="raw"` every field the old result had is identical to the old function's output (keep the old path; compare `rows`, `best`, `tied`, `chosen`, `versus`, `lomo`).
  - `test_choose_cli_objective_mean`: `choose.main([... "--objective", "mean"])` exits 0 and prints `objective mean`; an unknown objective is refused by `choose.choose` with ChooseError.

- Produces:
  - `ladder_half(truth, codes, start, stop) -> dict` of summable counts and the clean pairs' lists (IoUs, start errors, end errors) for one graded half;
  - `ladder_summary(halves) -> dict` pooled per truth: `n_gt`, `n_pred`, per IoU `tp` and the lost pairs by class (`clean`, `merge`, `split`, `both`), `spell_f1`, `clean_loss` (the clean-pair ladder loss), `split_loss`, `merge_loss`, `both_loss` (the same figure per class), `merge_share` (predicted spells overlapping ≥ 2 true spells of their team, of all predicted), `split_share` (true spells overlapping ≥ 2 predicted of their team, of all true), unmatched-at-0.3 counts by whether they overlap any spell of their team, the clean pairs' IoU median and share ≥ each IoU, the start and end errors' median, quartiles and share within ±5 frames;
  - `calibration(p, y, bins=10) -> dict`: `n`, `log_loss`, `brier`, `ece`, `reliability` (per bin: low, high, n, mean p, mean y), `alpha`, `beta` (Platt by maximum likelihood on x = logit(clip(p, 1e-6, 1 - 1e-6)), Newton iterations, deterministic);
  - `diagnose(config_path, profile="tracking", seeds=None, match_ids=None, matches=None, calibration=None, data_dir=None, drop_match_only=False, log=print) -> dict` (the report);
  - CLI `python pipeline/benchmark/diagnose.py CONFIG [--profile P] [--seeds N] [--matches ID ...] [--data-dir D] [--drop-match-only] [--no-write]`, writing `<data dir>/runs/diagnose/<stem>.json` unless `--no-write`, and printing the tables.

The definitions are A19 part 1's, exactly:
- Spells: `pm.possession_spells(pm.fill_abstentions(codes, [(start, stop)]), [(start, stop)])` and the same on the truth (as `score.score_halves`).
- Matched pairs: the greedy pass of `pm.matched_gt_flags` written out so that it also returns the (gt, pred, iou) pairs, at IoU 0.3. Pairs matched at θ are those with IoU ≥ θ (the same list cut at θ). Assert in the checks that the flags of this pass equal `pm.matched_gt_flags` at every θ in `score.IOUS`.
- Same-team overlap counts from `pm.spell_overlaps` (which lists only same-team overlapping pairs): for a predicted spell, the number of distinct true spells it overlaps; for a true spell, the number of distinct predicted ones.
- Class of a pair (g, p): clean if p overlaps 1 true and g overlaps 1 predicted; merge if p ≥ 2 and g = 1; split if g ≥ 2 and p = 1; both if both ≥ 2.
- `clean_loss` = mean over θ of 2 · (tp_θ + clean pairs lost at θ) / (n_gt + n_pred), minus the pooled spellF1; `split_loss`, `merge_loss`, `both_loss` the same with that class's lost pairs. The pooled spellF1 here must equal `score.pooled(...)["spell_f1_mean"]` for the same halves.
- Calibration, against the raw truth only: each source's `home` on graded frames where it is finite and the truth is H or A (y = truth == H); `shape_possession`-kind sources also split by `inputs.atd.ball_present` (visible / missing); each source's `alive` on graded frames where it is finite (y = truth != D). The posterior: for each graded block, `hmm.log_likelihoods` of every source windowed to the block (`Evidence.window`), `hmm.marginals` at the config's `[decoder]` `switch_team`, `switch_dead`, `min_possession`, `min_dead` (and `eps` if given); y_alive vs P(H)+P(A) on the block, on all graded frames and split by whether any source has a finite `home` on the frame (`team_speaks` / `team_silent`); y_home vs P(H)/(P(H)+P(A)) on the truth's alive frames. Refuse (BenchmarkError) a config whose decoder is not `hmm` or that sets `restart_source`.
- The report: `{"config": {"path", "stem", "sha256"}, "profile", "seeds", "matches", "dropped_match_only", "ladder": {"raw", "coarse", "mean": {"spell_f1", "clean_loss", "split_loss", "merge_loss", "both_loss"}}, "calibration": {source name: {...}, "posterior": {"alive", "home"}}, "git", "data_dir"}` via `run.write_json`; per-match figures are not needed.

### Task 3: the decoder's posterior edges (R025) — only after A19's gate passes

**Files:**
- Modify: `pipeline/decode/hmm.py`
- Test: `pipeline/selftest.py` (beside the other `test_hmm_*` checks)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `hmm.decode(..., edges="viterbi")`, `hmm.EDGES = ("viterbi", "posterior")`, `hmm.posterior_edges(labels, posterior) -> np.ndarray` (new int8 labels; the input is not written).

The rule, R025 part 1: for adjacent runs u = [a, b) and v = [b, c) with different labels, W = [b − (b − a − 1) // 2, b + (c − b − 1) // 2]. A frame t *favours v* when `posterior[t, v] >= posterior[t, u]` and `posterior[t, v] > 0`. If W's first frame does not favour v and some frame of W does, b′ = the first frame of W that favours v; otherwise (W's first frame already favours v, or none does) b′ = b. Also return, or make computable, the counts R025 reports: changes moved by kind (team, into D, out of D), |b′ − b|, and the two kinds left in place (crossing before W, after W) — e.g. `posterior_edges(labels, posterior, stats=None)` filling a dict when one is passed. All changes are computed from the Viterbi labels independently (the windows cannot overlap), then applied. `posterior` columns are H, A, D = the label codes (0, 1, 2).

In `decode`: validate `edges` (ValueError naming the allowed values); `edges="posterior"` with `restart_source` is refused (no four-state posterior), as `posterior=True` is. With `"posterior"`: labels from `viterbi`, `post = marginals(...)` once, labels = `posterior_edges(labels, post)`; if `posterior=True` too, return the same `post` array. Add a "Posterior edges" section to the module docstring (the belief, the window, the median reading, the cost: one `marginals` per block) and remove nothing else.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `pipeline/selftest.py`:
  - `test_hmm_edges_default_is_viterbi`: on the random blocks other hmm tests use, `decode(..., edges="viterbi")` equals `decode(...)` without the key, bit for bit.
  - `test_hmm_posterior_edges_hand_built`: a hand-built labels array and posterior where the move goes earlier and later inside W, where W's first frame already favours v (no move), and where no frame of W does (no move); a tie `P_v == P_u` favours v; `P_v == P_u == 0` does not.
  - `test_hmm_posterior_edges_never_lose_a_run`: 200 seeded random label arrays (runs of length 1 to 12 over H/A/D) with random row-normalised posteriors: the sequence of run labels is unchanged, every run keeps ≥ 1 frame, and every change moves within its W.
  - `test_hmm_posterior_edges_centre_a_stretch_without_evidence`: H evidence, then 40 frames with no evidence, then A evidence; Viterbi's change is on the stretch's first frame, the posterior edge within 1 frame of the stretch's middle.
  - `test_hmm_posterior_edges_refusals`: unknown `edges` value; `edges="posterior"` with `restart_source`.
