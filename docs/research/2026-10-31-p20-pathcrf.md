## 3. What the published model is, and its exact preprocessing

**The checkpoint.**
- Trial **140** = "Dynamic Masked CRF" (`crf_model: dynamic_sparse_crf`), the paper's headline model.
- The tutorial loads **`saved/140/model/state_dict_best_acc.pt`**.
- Architecture: `agent_model: set_tf`, `seq_model: sab`, `macro_type = micro_type = poss_edge`, `node_in_dim 8`,
  `team_size 11`, `target_fps 5`, `window_seconds 10`, `flip_pitch` true, `coarse_dim 16`, `fine_dim 64`,
  `seq_dim 128`, `crf_edge_dim 16`, 3,431,031 parameters.
- It was saved with `torch.save(module.state_dict(), …)`, a plain state dict, so `torch.load(..., weights_only=True)`
  loads it and no pickle code runs.

**Loading the tracking** (`datatools/sportec_data.py`, `preprocess.py`).
- `kloppy.sportec.load_tracking(raw_data=positions XML, meta_data=matchinformation XML, coordinates="sportec",
  only_alive=False)`, then `.transform(to_orientation=STATIC_HOME_AWAY,
  to_pitch_dimensions=MetricPitchDimensions(standardized=True, x 0–105, y 0–68))` and `.to_df()`.
- Player columns are renamed `home_<shirt>` / `away_<shirt>`.
- Rows where every player is NaN are dropped.
- `frame_id` stays kloppy's, which is DFL's frame number N (T30: kloppy's `frame_id` = our `frame_number`).

**Episodes** (`utils.label_frames_and_episodes`). An episode is a run of `ball_state == "alive"` frames per period;
alive frames less than 5 frames apart are merged. `episode_id` 0 is dead. Training and inference use the episodes only.

**Phases** (`utils.label_phases`). A phase is a stretch with a constant set of active players: it changes at every
substitution, red card and period start, and needs at least 100 alive frames. Per phase, `detect_keepers` takes, per
team, the node whose mean x is furthest from the halfway line. The **left team** is the one whose keeper has the
smaller mean x.

**Kinematics** (`utils.calculate_running_features`, at 25 Hz, per player per period).
- Outliers: for players, an instantaneous speed above 12 m/s sets the point to NaN, and it is filled by linear
  interpolation.
- `vx, vy = savgol_filter(diff(x)·25, 15, 2)`, `speed = |v|`.
- `accel = |savgol_filter(diff(v)·25, 9, 2)|`; the first and last accel are 0.

**Node features** (8, in order, with no further normalisation):
1. team flag: −1 left team, +1 right team, 0 outside node;
2. goalkeeper flag;
3. x (m);
4. y (m);
5. vx (m/s);
6. vy (m/s);
7. speed;
8. accel.

**Nodes** (26). They are ordered left team (≤ 11) + right team (≤ 11) + 4 outside nodes at fixed points with zero
velocity:
- `out_left` (0, 34);
- `out_right` (105, 34);
- `out_bottom` (52.5, 0);
- `out_top` (52.5, 68).

A team with fewer than 11 is padded with all-zero nodes (`<team>_pad_k`). The `player_mask` is built but
`SetLSTM.forward` never reads it.

**Teams.** Teams are handled by side, not by home and away. Training flips x and y at random and swaps the team blocks
when x flips, so the model learned "block 1 = the left team", not home or away.

**Sampling and windows** (`inference.inference_episode`).
- Every 5th frame from the episode's start, plus the episode's last frame, gives 5 fps.
- Windows are 50 steps (10 s) with a stride of 25 steps (50% overlap).
- Emissions and the dynamic CRF's edge embeddings are blended linearly over the overlaps.
- **One Viterbi runs per episode** over 576 compressed edge states:
  - 22 senders × 26 receivers;
  - plus 4 outside self-loops, which are **absorbing**.
- The decoded 5-fps state is held forward (`ffill`) to 25 fps.
- `correct_episode_lasts=True` forces an episode's last node from the next episode's first predicted set piece.

**Edges.** A self-loop A→A means A controls the ball. A→B (B ≠ A) is the ball in flight from A to B. A→out_X is the
ball going out. out_X→out_X is out of play.

**Missing data.** If any active node of an episode has a NaN in any feature, `inference_episode` **returns None and
the whole episode is skipped** (inference.py lines 406–407).

**What the repository does *not* handle.** There are no anonymous, fragmented or wrong-team tracks.
`datatools/preprocess_noise.py` perturbs positions only:
- occlusion merges players within 1 to 3 m;
- a per-episode bias of σ 0.5 to 2 m;
- outliers of σ 5 m at p 0.001 to 0.04.

Its `SCENARIOS` list is empty in the published file, the paper reports no noise experiment, and nothing drops,
fragments or swaps an identity. **So an adapter is needed**, and it must hand the model complete tracks inside in-play
segments.

## 8. Is the simplest adapter that fits the advisor's rules enough?

The advisor's rule 4 says:
- per 10 s window, slots by metadata team, with the wrong-team slots kept;
- an ending slot is joined to a beginning one, greedy by smallest gap, at most 5 m and 2 s;
- at most 11 per team, with the extras that have the fewest frames dropped;
- 5 fps, each step held 5 frames.

**Structurally yes, with four completions the model forces.** Each must be declared in part 1 and signed off by the
advisor (they are choices the advisor's text does not make):

- **D1, segmentation.** The model was trained and is run inside in-play episodes only. Its outside nodes are
  absorbing, so a window that starts in dead time can stay "out of play" through the restart and the play after it.
  Truth episodes would put the truth into the inputs, which the benchmark's design forbids (`inputs.py`: "No truth
  through the inputs").
  - **Proposed:** windows tile the runs that `main.toml` decodes as H or A on that match and seed. These are
    label-free, and they are step 1's own segmentation on BAR-ATH.
  - Each run is cut into ⌈L/250⌉ windows of near-equal length (at most 250 frames, so 10 s). Runs shorter than
    16 frames get no window: `savgol(15)` needs 16.
- **D2, complete tracks.** The model needs no NaN (rule: the whole episode is skipped).
  - **Proposed:** after the joins, a join's gap is filled by linear interpolation, and a node's frames before its
    first or after its last observed frame inside the window are held at that position.
  - Their own kinematics (`calculate_running_features`, with its 12 m/s outlier repair) then run on each window's
    node tracks.
- **D3, per-window inference.** Node identities differ between windows, so their 50% overlap blending and the
  episode-wide Viterbi cannot be used. **Proposed:** each window is its own episode for `inference_episode`: one
  model pass and one CRF Viterbi per window, `last_node=None`. This is how the model was trained: 10 s windows, the
  CRF loss per window.
- **D4, frames and team mapping.**
  - **The team.** A decoded edge's team is its **sender's** (the self-loop's player, or the passer while the ball is
    in flight, which is DFL's flag convention). A sender that is an outside node is out of play and counts as
    **wrong** (rule 5). A pad node takes its block's team. The mapping goes by node name (`home_k` / `away_k`), never
    by block, because the blocks are left and right.
  - **The frame set.** Rule 5's frames are graded, truth H/A, and the seed's served ball missing. **Proposed primary:**
    rule 5's frames **∩ `main.toml` decodes H or A**. Neither model is charged for `main.toml`'s dead-ball errors,
    which is the advisor's own rule for P19's comparator. The literal rule-5 set, with PathCRF's silent frames counted
    wrong, is reported beside it.
  - **Coordinates.** x × 105, y × 68 (their standard pitch).

The empirical question, how much it loses to hold-filled phantoms, joins and wrong-team nodes, is what step 0
measures. The scorer must report accuracy by window class (with a join; with a team over 11; neither) for both
models, because the pre-approved retry (advisor 3.3) depends on it.

1. **Reproduction may fail on versions.** Their requirements are unpinned and incomplete, so kloppy, pandas or torch
   may differ from theirs. The 06:30 rule bounds it: fixing usage is allowed until then, each fix is named in part 2,
   and no retry is spent.
2. **kloppy's memory on the 373 MB XML** is unmeasured (estimated 4–6 GB). Run the clean step alone, with a
   free-memory guard of ≥ 8 GB.
3. **The heavy queue order puts P20 last.** P22, then P21 step 0, then P19, then P20. The clean run (10–20 min)
   decides the 06:30 rule, so it should get the first free slot of about 20 min.
4. **The convention gap.** The 92.4% is against event-split possession, not DFL's flag, so even the clean accuracy on
   our truth may disappoint. That is a finding, not a usage error.
5. **D1's segmentation reads `main.toml`.** A `main.toml` change by P22 or P19 tonight would move PathCRF's windows.
   Pin `main.toml`'s sha256 in part 1, and prepare from that file.
6. **Security.** Their `.py` files run in our process, so they are pinned by blob SHA-1 and sha256. Load the
   checkpoint with `weights_only=True`. A one-time read of the imported files' top-level code (no network, no writes
   outside the work dir) belongs in the review.
